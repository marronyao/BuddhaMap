About the project

Built for an open, connected Europe.

BuddhaMap is being designed as a practical, welcoming directory for people of every tradition — and for anyone simply curious. Listings will bring addresses, official links, context and community knowledge into one calm place.

Our intention
“Less searching.
More arriving.”
